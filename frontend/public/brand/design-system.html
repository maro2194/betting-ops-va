Logo Usage

Do / Don't guidelines

Maintain minimum clear space of 0.5× chip height on all sides. At 120px, this equals 60px of breathing room.

0.5× height 0.5×
DO — Dark variant on dark background
Use dark variant on any surface with #0a0a0f or similar dark base.
DON'T — Dark variant on light background
wrong variant
Dark chip fill (#0d1117) disappears on white. Use light variant instead.
DO — Light variant on light background
Light variant uses #fafcfa chip fill with #00a37a stroke — readable on any light surface.
DON'T — Logo on colored/branded backgrounds
clashing bg
Never place the logo on colored backgrounds (purple, red, etc). Use only on brand bg colors.
